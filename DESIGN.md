# Design System: TrackFit (Titanium & Vermilion Editorial Precision)

## Overview

**Creative Direction: "Titanium & Vermilion Precision Telemetry"**

TrackFit is a light-first, architectural fitness platform designed with high-density editorial clarity. It utilizes a cool titanium vapor canvas (`#f4f5f7`), crisp white surfaces (`#ffffff`), pitch graphite typography (`#111827`), cool steel hairline borders (`#e5e7eb`), and a high-contrast signal accent (**Racing Vermilion `#ea580c`**) used with strict restraint (~10% surface area).

## Colors

- **Canvas Background**: Cool Vapor White `#f4f5f7`
- **Surface / Panels**: Pure White `#ffffff`
- **Subtle Surface**: Light Steel `#f3f4f6`
- **Hairline Border**: Steel `#e5e7eb` (zinc-200 / gray-200)
- **Primary Text**: Pitch Graphite `#111827`
- **Secondary Text**: Muted Steel `#4b5563`
- **Muted Text**: Light Slate `#9ca3af`
- **Primary Signal Accent**: Racing Vermilion `#ea580c` (Restrained 10% usage)
- **Success Accent**: Active Botanical Green `#16a34a`
- **Warning Accent**: Dark Ochre `#ca8a04`
- **Error Accent**: Deep Rose Carmine `#be123c`

## Anti-Patterns (STRICTLY BANNED)
- NO blue, purple, or indigo primary accent colors.
- NO dark pitch-black background gradients.
- NO glassmorphism, background blur overlays, or glowing halo effects.
- NO generic identical card-stack layouts.
- NO decorative blobs or artificial sparkles.

## Typography
- Display / Headers: Inter / System Sans, Heavy / Black Weight (900/800), Tight Tracking (`-0.03em`)
- Body / Data: Inter / System Sans, Medium / Bold Weight (500/700)
